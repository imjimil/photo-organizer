# Opal — Product

## Register

product (personal tool, editorial quality)

## Users

One primary user: a collector of quote screenshots, literary passages, and mood images who wants to **browse**, **feel**, and **find** — not manage files like a DAM system.

## Product Purpose

Opal is a local-first gallery for a personal library of ~25k quote and literary images. Success means:

1. **Browse** feels like scrolling a curated feed — effortless, beautiful, no friction.
2. **Search by vibe** finds images that *feel* right even when words fail.
3. **Search by quote** finds exact or near text once OCR completes.
4. The app feels **intentionally designed** — never generic, never "AI made this."

## Brand Personality

Quiet, literary, iridescent. Opal shifts with angle — the app should feel the same: mood-forward, text-aware, personal. Not a startup dashboard. Not a social network.

Three words: **intimate, literate, luminous**.

## Anti-references

- Purple-to-blue gradients, neon glows, glassmorphism cards
- Inter/Roboto/system-ui as the only personality
- Card nested in card nested in card
- Gray `#666` text on colored backgrounds
- Hero metrics, "boost productivity" SaaS copy
- Filename-first UI (users care about quotes and images, not `2023-01-01_71744...jpg`)

## Core flows

1. **Feed** — open app, infinite masonry scroll, discover
2. **Search** — type vibe or quote, results with similarity when relevant
3. **Lightbox** — tap image, read full quote, "more like this", copy text
4. **Filter** — has text, date range (secondary, not blocking browse)

## Accessibility

WCAG 2.1 AA. Keyboard navigable lightbox. `prefers-reduced-motion` respected. Focus rings visible.
