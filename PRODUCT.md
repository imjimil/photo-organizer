# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Designers, photographers, and collectors sitting at a desktop monitor, hunting through a local archive of photos, screenshots, and moodboards. The job is to find one image in seconds without leaving the machine or uploading anything.

## Product Purpose

Opal is a local Google Images: search, browse, and collect a private library that stays on disk. Success is a quiet, dense desktop tool where the pictures are the interface and chrome never competes with them.

## Brand Personality

Dry, precise, unsentimental. Three words: focused, material, fast. The voice is instructional, never cute. No greetings, no "prints," no darkroom poetry.

## Anti-references

- SaaS bento dashboards, hero metrics, and greeting cards
- Photo-app darkroom gold on black
- Google-blue search chrome
- Floating glass pill nav, full-radius buttons, decorative blobs
- Editorial serif wordmarks and ALL CAPS eyebrows
- Pinterest masonry cards with drop shadows and hover lift

## Design Principles

1. Images first. Chrome recedes; the grid and the search field do the work.
2. Desktop conventions are features. Sidebar, titlebar, keyboard, density.
3. Search is the product. Finding is the default, not a secondary tab.
4. Familiar controls, distinctive material. Standard affordances, uncommon color and type.
5. Motion reports state. No choreography that delays a search.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast on chrome and text. Visible focus rings. Full keyboard path for search, grid, and viewer. Honor `prefers-reduced-motion`. Touch targets stay 44px on compact layouts; desktop chrome can be visually tighter with padded hit areas.
